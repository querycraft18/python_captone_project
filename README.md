Project Overview
The Sales Performance & Customer Insights Dashboard is a Streamlit-based data analytics project designed to monitor and analyze business sales performance and customer purchasing behavior. It aims to provide meaningful insights into revenue, profitability, product performance, regional sales, customer spending, and order cancellations.
The dashboard covers the period from January 2024 to January 2026, involving six regions, three sales channels, and eight product categories. Through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs), the project helps identify sales trends, understand customer behavior, evaluate business performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

Executive Summary
•	The dashboard analyzes sales performance and customer purchasing behavior from January 2024 to January 2026.
•	The business generated a total revenue of ₱5.37 million from 11,439 orders.
•	The average order value (AOV) reached ₱434.35, indicating the average amount spent per order.
•	The business recorded a total profit of approximately ₱1.46 million.
•	The overall profit margin was 29.3%, reflecting a positive level of profitability.
•	Monthly revenue remained relatively consistent throughout the analysis period, with several increases and decreases.
•	KidJoy Toys emerged as the highest-performing product in the product performance chart.
•	Central Luzon recorded the highest regional revenue among the six regions displayed.
•	Returning customers accounted for 74.1% of the customer base, while new customers represented 25.9%.
•	Customer spending distribution showed that most purchases were concentrated in the lower spending ranges, with fewer high-value transactions.
•	The monthly order cancellation trend fluctuated throughout the reporting period.
•	The overall cancellation rate was 8.23%, indicating an opportunity to improve order fulfillment and customer experience.
•	The findings provide a basis for improving sales strategies, customer retention, product management, and operational efficiency.

Insights
The dashboard shows that the business generated ₱5.37 million in total revenue from 11,439 orders, with approximately ₱1.46 million in profit and a 29.3% profit margin, indicating positive financial performance. Monthly revenue remained relatively stable despite periodic fluctuations, suggesting consistent sales activity throughout the reporting period. KidJoy Toys emerged as the top-performing product, while Central Luzon recorded the highest regional revenue, highlighting their significant contributions to overall sales. Customer analysis shows that 74.1% are returning customers, reflecting strong customer retention, although the 25.9% share of new customers indicates opportunities for customer acquisition. Additionally, most transactions fall within lower spending ranges, suggesting potential to increase the average order value of ₱434.35. The 8.23% cancellation rate also highlights an operational concern that may affect completed sales and customer satisfaction. Overall, the findings demonstrate a profitable business with opportunities to strengthen sales performance, expand its customer base, and improve operational efficiency.

Recommendations
 business should maintain its profitability by regularly monitoring revenue, operating expenses, and profit margins while using monthly sales trends to improve forecasting and planning. High-performing products, particularly KidJoy Toys, should receive priority in inventory management and promotional activities, while lower-performing products should be evaluated for possible improvements in pricing and marketing. Regional strategies should focus on maintaining strong sales in Central Luzon while introducing targeted promotions and marketing campaigns in regions with lower revenue contributions. To strengthen customer retention and acquisition, the business should implement loyalty rewards, personalized discounts, referral programs, and first-time purchase incentives. Increasing the average order value can also be encouraged through product bundles, cross-selling, and minimum-purchase discounts. Furthermore, the business should investigate the causes of order cancellations and improve inventory accuracy, order processing, payment coordination, and delivery communication. Finally, the dashboard should be updated regularly and enhanced with additional performance indicators to help management monitor progress, identify emerging trends, and make informed business decisions
